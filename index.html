what u learning here?

Sesame seeds are small, oval-shaped edible seeds that come from the sesame plant (Sesamum indicum). They are one of the oldest cultivated oilseed crops and are available in white, black and brown varieties. They have a mild, nutty flavour and are widely used in cooking around the world. Sesame seeds can be sprinkled over rice, noodles, salads and bread to add flavour and texture. They are also used to produce sesame oil and tahini, a smooth paste commonly found in hummus and sauces. Nutritionally, sesame seeds contain healthy fats, protein, fibre and minerals such as calcium, iron, magnesium and zinc. These nutrients support bone health, digestion and normal body functions. Overall, sesame seeds are a versatile ingredient used in meals, snacks and desserts worldwide.
